Pairing rules

7 rows

RuleDetail
PairingEach reference clip has a matching target clip (empty scene -> scene with bird)
MatchReference and target must have the same length and resolution
Split domainsKeep indoor pairs and outdoor pairs SEPARATE
SkipPeople, text, watermarks, other animals (unless that is the bird you want)
CameraPrefer locked-off shots or slow pans
Start sizeAbout 20-40 clean pairs per domain (sky, then house) beats hundreds of messy clips
Indoor targetsTrue house-interior bird clips are rare. Use cage/pet parrot clips, film a pet bird, or composite a bird onto empty rooms
