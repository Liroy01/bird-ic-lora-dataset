How to collect

RoleSceneWhat is in the clipWhat is NOT in the clipWhy
ReferenceOutdoorEmpty sky (clear, cloudy, sunset, overcast)Birds, planes, text, peopleBefore state for sky birds
TargetOutdoorSame kind of sky WITH the bird flying / glidingExtra animals, logosAfter state for sky birds
ReferenceIndoorEmpty or almost-empty house room, ideally with a windowPeople, pets, birdsBefore state for indoor birds
TargetIndoorSame room WITH the bird on sofa, lamp, sill, or flying acrossPeople, other animalsAfter state so the bird is IN the house, not outside
Extra varietyBothLiving room, bedroom, kitchen, hallway; day and nightWatermarks, heavy camera shakeStops the LoRA from only working on one room / one sky

Pairing rules

RuleDetail
PairingEach reference clip has a matching target clip (empty scene -> scene with bird)
MatchReference and target must have the same length and resolution
Split domainsKeep indoor pairs and outdoor pairs SEPARATE
SkipPeople, text, watermarks, other animals, or a bird already in the sky
CameraPrefer locked-off shots or slow pans
Start sizeAbout 20-40 clean pairs per domain (sky, then house) beats hundreds of messy clips
Indoor targetsRare on stock sites. Film a pet/stuffed bird, composite a bird onto empty rooms, or search parrot indoor / budgie living room / bird on window sill
